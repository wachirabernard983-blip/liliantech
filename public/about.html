ABOUT LILIANTECH

About LilianTech

A straightforward survey and rewards platform designed around clarity, fair participation and a human experience.

What we do

LilianTech connects members with survey and research opportunities that match their available profile information. We aim to make the experience simple: create an account, review opportunities, complete eligible surveys and track rewards in one place.

Our approach

We believe online earning platforms should explain how they work instead of making users guess. We keep important information easy to find, use clear language and show members the activity associated with their account.

Built for real people

The platform is designed to work comfortably on phones, tablets and desktop computers. We focus on readable layouts, sensible spacing and predictable navigation so that moving between pages feels consistent.
