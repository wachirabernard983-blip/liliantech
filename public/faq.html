Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions about accounts, surveys and rewards.

How do surveys work?

Available surveys are shown in your account when they are available to you. Eligibility can vary by survey, and completing a survey successfully records the applicable reward.

Is registration free?

Yes. Creating a LilianTech account is free.

When are new surveys released?

When a member completes the currently available survey batch, the platform uses a 30-minute release cycle before making another batch available.

Do I need to stay logged in?

No. The survey release process is handled on the server. You can return later and check your dashboard for newly available opportunities.

Where can I see my earnings?

Your completed survey rewards are shown in the Earnings section of your dashboard.

Why might a survey disappear?

Survey availability can change when eligibility rules, capacity or completion limits are reached.
