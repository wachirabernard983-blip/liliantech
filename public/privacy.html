Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

1. Information we collect

When you create an account, we collect information such as your name, email address, password credentials, account activity, survey activity, earnings and payout details you choose to provide.

2. How we use information

We use information to operate your account, match you with available research opportunities, record verified rewards, process withdrawals, prevent fraud and provide support.

3. Research and survey partners

When you participate in a survey, relevant information may be shared with the research or survey provider responsible for that opportunity so it can determine eligibility, conduct the survey and validate completion. We only enable integrations that are approved for LilianTech.

4. Payments and withdrawals

If you request a withdrawal, the payout information you provide is used to process and verify that request. We do not ask for passwords to your external payment accounts.

5. Cookies and security

We use necessary cookies and server-side sessions to keep accounts signed in and secure. We also use technical and fraud-prevention measures to protect the platform and research partners.

6. Data retention

We retain account, transaction and compliance information for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, resolve disputes, meet security requirements and comply with applicable obligations.

7. Your choices

You may contact us about your personal information, account or privacy questions. Where applicable, you may request access, correction or deletion subject to legal and operational requirements.

8. Contact

All LilianTech communication is handled through support@liliantech.online.
